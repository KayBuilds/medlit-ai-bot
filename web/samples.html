Cardiology Samples

SGLT2 Inhibitors in Heart Failure: Real-World Evidence from Indian Cohort

📰 Indian Heart Journal 👤 Cardiologists 📊 High (RCT)
Key Finding
Retrospective analysis of 1,200 HF patients showed 34% reduction in hospitalization with dapagliflozin vs standard care. Benefits seen across EF categories including HFpEF.
Clinical Impact
🔴 High - Practice-changing evidence for broad SGLT2 use in HF
Action Item
Consider SGLT2i for all HF patients regardless of EF unless contraindicated. Check eGFR thresholds.

Triple Therapy in AF and Diabetes: GLP-1RA + SGLT2i + Metformin

📰 European Heart Journal 👤 General Cardiology 📊 High (Meta-analysis)
Key Finding
Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (n=45,000) shows triple therapy reduces MACE by 42% vs dual therapy in T2DM with AF. Stroke risk reduced by 38%.
Clinical Impact
🔴 High - Significant stroke/MACE reduction in high-risk population
Action Item
For T2DM + AF patients on OAC, consider adding GLP-1RA if not already on triple therapy.

Endocrinology Samples

Time-Restricted Eating vs Calorie Counting: 12-Month Outcomes in T2DM

📰 Diabetes Care 👤 Endocrinologists / Diabetologists 📊 High (RCT)
Key Finding
8-hour TRE window (12pm-8pm) achieved similar HbA1c reduction (-1.2%) as calorie restriction but with 40% better adherence and greater visceral fat loss.
Clinical Impact
🟡 Moderate - Viable alternative for patients struggling with calorie counting
Action Item
Offer TRE as alternative lifestyle intervention, especially for patients with busy schedules or who dislike calorie tracking.

Continuous Glucose Monitoring vs HbA1c: Hypoglycemia Detection in Type 1

📰 JCEM 👤 Endocrinologists 📊 High (Prospective)
Key Finding
CGM identified 7.4x more hypoglycemic episodes (<70 mg/dL) than HbA1c monitoring alone. 42% of episodes were nocturnal and asymptomatic.
Clinical Impact
🔴 High - CGM superiority for safety in T1DM established
Action Item
Advocate CGM for all T1DM patients. Review Time in Range metrics alongside HbA1c.
